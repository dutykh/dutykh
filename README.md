# Denys Dutykh

I am an applied mathematician in the Mathematics Department of Khalifa University of Science and Technology, in Abu
Dhabi. My work runs from nonlinear water waves and the numerical methods that compute them, through the spectra of
black holes and wormholes, to questions of number theory settled with the help of exact computation. The repositories
below hold the codes and the data of my articles: each one says what it computes, which article it accompanies and how
to cite it, and the button "Cite this repository" on its page gives the reference.

## Water waves and numerical methods

| Repository | What it computes |
|---|---|
| [SSGW](https://github.com/dutykh/SSGW) | steady periodic gravity waves of any steepness in any depth, by the Babenko equation |
| [NavierStokes2D](https://github.com/dutykh/NavierStokes2D) | the two-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations on a periodic square, for teaching |
| [BabenkoSolitaryWave](https://github.com/dutykh/BabenkoSolitaryWave) | solitary gravity waves of the full Euler equations |
| [BabenkoCG](https://github.com/dutykh/BabenkoCG) | generalised solitary capillary-gravity waves |

| [SerreGravityWave](https://github.com/dutykh/SerreGravityWave) | the Serre–Green–Naghdi equations by a pseudo-spectral method |

| [FlowReconstruct](https://github.com/dutykh/FlowReconstruct) | the potential flow under a free surface, from Dirichlet data |
| [sineGordonGraph](https://github.com/dutykh/sineGordonGraph), [sineGordon_FreeFem](https://github.com/dutykh/sineGordon_FreeFem) | the sine-Gordon equation on networks |
| [Feller](https://github.com/dutykh/Feller) | Feller's diffusion equation by a Lagrangian scheme with moving nodes |
| [SerreCyl](https://github.com/dutykh/SerreCyl) | weakly dispersive long-wave models of pulsatile blood flow |
| [NEOLAB](https://github.com/dutykh/NEOLAB) | the negative electrode of lead-acid batteries, in Scilab |
